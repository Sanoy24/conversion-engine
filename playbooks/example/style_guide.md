# Acme Engineering — Outbound Style Guide

| Principle | What it means | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Clear, brief, actionable. | No filler or excessive pleasantries. The subject line states the intent. |
| Grounded | Every claim is verifiable. | Reference only facts from the signal brief. When a signal is weak, ask instead of asserting. |
| Honest | Never over-claim. | Do not describe hiring as "aggressive" without strong evidence. Never promise capacity we have not confirmed. |
| Professional | Written for founders, CTOs, and VPs Engineering. | Plain language, no internal jargon. |
| Humble | Findings, not judgements. | Present competitor gaps as research observations, never as failures of the reader's team. |

## Structure of a cold email

1. One sentence that shows we did our homework (a specific, public signal).
2. One sentence on why that signal usually matters.
3. One question or one ask — never both, never more than one.
4. Signature.
